# Advanced Lawnmower Simulator: Playdate Edition

## A tribute to the most POWERFUL and ACCURATE lawn mowing game of the 80's!

This is a 'tribute' to the original Advanced Lawnmower Simulator on the ZX Spectrum, the April Fool game that 'Your Sinclair' magazine once published on their cover tape & was written by the legendary Duncan MacDonald (RIP).

Choose from several different machines of lawn mowing prowess in order to 'go get that grass'.

Developed for the [Playdate](https://play.date) as part of [#RaeJam2025](https://itch.io/jam/rae-jam), which coincided with the ZX Spectrum's release 43 years prior & the original game's appearance in the April 1988 issue of [YS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Your_Sinclair).

The project aimed to be incredibly faithful to the [original 1980s 'game'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advanced_Lawnmower_Simulator) whilst adding some enhancements such as Crank-To-Mow™ & mowing statistics for your overall gardening pleasure.

EVERY platform needs a version of this game, so here's the absolute, definite (only?) Playdate Edition!

🏆 Supports [Playdate Achievements](https://playdatesquad.github.io/pd-achievements)! 35 achievements to unlock in the game & viewable through a dedicated achievement app such as [Trophy Case](https://gurtt.dev/trophy-case).
